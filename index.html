01 ABOUT TIME VILLAGE

A PLACE TO RECONNECT

Life in a more
natural rhythm.

Time Village and Resorts Private Limited envisions an integrated hospitality and residential destination at Anjap, Karjat, Raigad district, Maharashtra.

Spread across approximately 55.36 acres, Time Village is conceived as a place where nature, wellness, recreation and community come together.

Inspired by the concept of Blue Zones, the vision is to encourage a balanced lifestyle through physical activity, mindful nutrition, meaningful social connections and a closer relationship with nature.
